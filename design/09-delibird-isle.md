> **Provenance & the reframe.** The original seed
> (`sources/chatgpt-anthropocene-design-export.txt`, lines 1–51) designed
> "Delibird Island" as a *lighthearted optional side-area* and asked one
> open question: side-content, or a **mandatory story chapter tied to the
> Climate Corps narrative** (rising seas, melting permafrost)? **Canon has
> answered: mandatory** (§00, §01). So §09's job is to keep every warm,
> whimsical detail the seed proposed — *and let the permafrost thaw run
> underneath all of it,* so the cheer reads as courage, not denial, and on a
> second visit reads as something closer to a wake.

## Canonical frame (LOCKED — from §00/§01/§06)

- **Zone 6 of Skaldmere.** Reached mid-game by **ferry/surf** after an earlier
  certification (§01). Not optional. An **interlude**, not a certification
  zone (no Delibird Isle credential).
- **The title location.** The whole game is named for this island — "titled
  for its quietest, most emblematic place, not its largest" (§00). Whatever
  §09 becomes is, in a sense, the game's thesis statement.
- **Festival cheer over a permafrost crisis.** The disturbance is **permafrost
  thaw** (§06), driven by **Mereholt Permafrost** (§05). The cheer is
  *partly Mereholt's money* — Foundation-funded festival banners and
  storm-relief blankets (§05). Irreversible damage (carbon feedback, §06)
  staged under string lights.
- **The thesis in miniature: give vs. take.** Delibird = **give/steward**
  (§00 mascot lock) — mutual aid as a survival strategy against a hostile
  environment. This is the explicit counter-thesis to extraction's **take.**
  The island is where the player *feels* the whole game's moral in one warm,
  doomed place.

## Geography (from the seed, retained)

Roughly **crescent-shaped — like a feather or wing** (the Delibird motif),
snowy peaks at the center, warmer beaches at the edges. Four areas:

| Area | Seed concept | Reframed load |
|------|--------------|---------------|
| **Snowy Summit** | wind-swept ice highlands, Ice-types | where the thaw is most visible — thermokarst ponds, slumping ground, the tilted "drunken forest" (§06) |
| **Holiday Village** | string lights, candy-colored cabins, a **gift square** where villagers trade | the social heart; the gift economy made literal; storm-relief culture (and the Foundation blankets) |
| **Shoreline Cliffs** | warmer beaches, Flying & Water Pokémon | the warming edge — species that shouldn't be this far north yet are (range shift, §06) |
| **Crystal Cavern** | ice-crystal side cave, rarer Ice Pokémon, a "Delibird boss event" | the cold refuge; the deepest cold the island has left; a candidate for the chapter's quiet climax |

## Ecology (per §06 — the cold-specialist zone)

- **Indicator read:** cold specialists compressed and physical thaw-signs.
  Candidate species (illustrative; full roster → §11): **Delibird**
  (signature), **Spheal** line, **Snorunt**, **Cubchoo**, Swinub line;
  **Wingull/Pelipper** at the shore; **Lapras** (surf), **Glalie** (deep
  cave) as rarer encounters. Festive trainers favor **Delibird, Stantler,**
  and other cold/"festive" species.
- **The thaw is the story.** Physical indicators (slumping village
  foundations, thermokarst, the drunken forest) are environmental storytelling
  the player *reads* the way they read the culvert in §08. **Irreversible**
  (§06) — this is the zone where "we can fix it later" is most plainly false.
- **Why the islanders are less captured than Harland's community** (§05): they
  have less to lose *to* Mereholt and more to lose *from* the thaw, so they're
  more open to Wren than the fishing community was.

## Gameplay & side content (DRAFT — reframed from the seed)

The seed's whimsical mechanics survive, but each one now carries the give/take
theme rather than just being bonus content:

- **Gift Exchange (the gift square).** NPCs offer randomized trades — give a
  common item, receive a random (sometimes rare) gift. *Reframe:* this is the
  **give economy** as a literal mechanic — the island runs on reciprocity, the
  opposite of extraction. Keep it warm; let it quietly *mean* something.
- **The Delibird Delivery questline.** Help Delibird deliver gifts/supplies to
  people scattered across the island; completion grants a special reward
  (upgraded TM Present, Lucky Egg, or a rare Egg). *Reframe:* the elder's lore
  — **Delibird as guardians who deliver supplies during storms** (§00's
  storm-relief lock) — makes the delivery quest a re-enactment of the island's
  survival ethic. The "storms" are getting worse.
- **The Climate Corps mini-mission.** Help the islanders handle **thawing
  permafrost releasing water into the village** — ferry sandbags/icy blocks
  with Delibird. *Reframe:* this is the **§06 thaw made playable** and the
  literal hinge between the cheerful surface and the locked crisis. It should
  not be fully solvable — you reduce harm, you don't "win" (Hale's law).
- **The Delibird Festival.** On the story beat, the village holds a winter
  festival — NPCs dance, Delibird scatter presents as hidden items.
  *Reframe:* the festival is the **cheerful+eerie contrast device** (§00) at
  full volume — genuine joy *and* Foundation-sponsored banners *and* a thaw
  underfoot. Play it straight and warm; the dread is in what the player now
  knows, not in the staging.

## Rewards (from the seed, retained)

A free **Delibird** gift Pokémon (possibly holding an exclusive item — e.g. an
"Everfrost Feather"); access to mid-game Ice-types; presents hidden across the
island; a **Ranger commendation** for the delivery mini-mission.

- *Note:* the commendation is a grace note, not a certification — Delibird Isle
  grants no credential (§01).

## How the breather carries the whole game

| Surface (what it feels like) | Load (what it means) |
|------------------------------|----------------------|
| Warmest, most generous people in Skaldmere | the **give** thesis; the counter to extraction |
| String lights, festival, presents | cheerful+eerie device (§00); partly Mereholt-funded (§05) |
| "Delibird deliver supplies during storms" | storm-relief lore; the storms are worsening (§06) |
| Help fix the village flooding | permafrost thaw, **irreversible** (§06); reduce harm, don't win |
| A relaxing side-island | the **title location** — the game's thesis in miniature (§00) |
