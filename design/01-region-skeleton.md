# 01 — Region & Journey Skeleton

> Status: **DRAFT** (revisable). An island region; each zone is an ecosystem,
> each major zone grants a **Ranger Certification** (the "challenge, not
> badges" progression), and each carries a distinct ecological pressure that
> ladders up toward the extraction endgame.

~6–7 certifications, large region, ecologically ordered.

## Design principles

- **Certifications are competencies, not type-gyms.** Each tests an ecological
  skill (survey, restraint, response, remediation), not "beat the Water gym."
- **Each zone escalates the same theme**, not the spectacle: damage that wears
  the mask of improvement; livelihoods entangled with harm; documentation as
  the only real power the player holds.
- **Pacing:** zones 1–2 teach restraint (locked). 3–5 widen the world and make
  restraint *harder* — doing nothing stops being acceptable. 6 is the emotional
  breather that reframes the stakes. 7–8 are the reckoning.
- **Delibird Isle placement** matches the original notes: reached mid-game by
  ferry/surf after an earlier certification; a "breather" that is secretly the
  whole game in miniature.
