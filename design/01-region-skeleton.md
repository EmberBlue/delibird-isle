## The region: Skaldmere (LOCKED)

The overall region is **Skaldmere** — a large, cold-temperate maritime
island region spanning saltmarsh and temperate forest in the south up to
thawing permafrost in the highlands and on Delibird Isle. The name fuses
*skald* (the Norse poet whose work was to **record** what would otherwise be
lost — the exact act at the moral center of the game: observe, document, the
Smeargle/notebook loop) with *mere* (a lake or wetland — the habitat that is
dying). **The region is named for the verb Wren comes to learn and the
ground that's vanishing beneath it.**

- **Skaldmere ≠ Delibird Isle.** Delibird Isle is one *location within*
  Skaldmere (zone 6 below) — the festive, chilly island whose cheer hides a
  permafrost crisis. The game is titled for its quietest, most emblematic
  place, not its largest (§00).

## The back half: the mentor reveal & the climax (LOCKED)

The three-layer reveal of Dr. Heron (§04) is paced one layer per late zone, a
slow burn that turns belief into betrayal into grief:

- **Zones 1–6 — Silencing.** The world (and Wren) believe Dr. Heron was
  buried for getting too close. The whole front of the game runs on this. The
  prologue's wounded **Poliwag** at the bridge (§00) is the silent seed: the
  species her noble lie once protected, witnessed before its meaning is known.
- **Zone 7 — Collaboration.** At the **Industrial Coast**, where the
  extraction economy becomes visible, Wren discovers Dr. Heron's resilience
  models are *inside* the greenwash. Betrayal. The assumed martyr was a
  collaborator.
- **Zone 8 — Coercion (full reveal) & climax.** The keystone habitat at the
  heart of **The Last Corridor** is itself a small, dying freshwater marsh —
  the thing the whole region depends on — with the **Poliwag line** as its
  keystone-indicator species. Wren finds **Dr. Heron alive there, in
  voluntary exile,** cataloging by hand the very species her falsified
  recovery data once "saved" (§04). The final layer lands face to face: she
  didn't sell out, she was *forced.* Partial confession; she has prepared her
  own end; Wren chooses to expose her anyway — exposing the greenwash means
  exposing her lie and de-protecting the Mahogany preserve it saved (the
  climax knot, §04). Truth-telling with maximum collateral. The
  clear-eyed-grief ending (§02).

This fuses zone 8's "keystone habitat everything depends on" with the
exile-wetland, the confession, and the climax into a single location — and
makes the deep-wilderness keystone a *freshwater marsh*, which both fits the
Poliwag line and mirrors the wetland south of Goldenrod where Sketch died.

## Design principles

- **Certifications are competencies, not type-gyms.** Each tests an ecological
  skill (survey, restraint, response, remediation), not "beat the Water gym."
- **Each zone escalates the same theme**, not the spectacle: damage that wears
  the mask of improvement; livelihoods entangled with harm; documentation as
  the only real power the player holds.
- **Pacing:** zones 1–2 teach restraint (locked). 3–5 widen the world and make
  restraint *harder* — doing nothing stops being acceptable. 6 is the emotional
  breather that reframes the stakes. 7–8 are the reckoning.
- **Delibird Isle placement** matches the original notes: reached mid-game by
  ferry/surf after an earlier certification; a "breather" that is secretly the
  whole game in miniature.
