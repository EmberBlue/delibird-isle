# Saltwick -- the arrival town on the Saltmarsh Coast (zone 1), a brick
# harbour town on a canal. The stone pier and its landing are where they always were
# (the arrival scene and the ferry warps land on them); everything else is new.
#
# North to south: the fir cap with the preserve avenue; the Annex and its weather plot,
# the row houses and the chandlery on the west bank; the walled canal with a plank
# footbridge, the Main Street bridge and the quay bridge; the Center, the fishers' hall,
# the fountain square and the inn on the east bank; Main Street (a kerbed road); a ledge
# with stairs down to the quay district; the quay, two rows deep; the harbour.
# Working quay: the mail ferry ties up west of the stone pier. The red-thread
# stake marks the spent oyster ground; the freight derrick stands on the east wharf.
